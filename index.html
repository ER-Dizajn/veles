How we work

From first measurement to finished room

Four steps, one team. You always know what happens next and who is doing it.

Consultation & measuring

We come to your space, take exact measurements and talk through how you actually use the room.

3D design

You see your kitchen or wardrobe in 3D before a single panel is cut — and we adjust until it is right.

CNC production

Panels are cut, drilled and edge-banded on CNC machines in our own workshop, to the millimetre.

Delivery & installation

Our own team delivers, fits everything on site, clears up and hands over a finished room.

Why us

What you actually get when you order from us

Truly made to measure

Nothing off the shelf. Every piece is built for your exact walls, ceilings and sloped roofs.

Our own workshop

We manufacture what we sell, so quality, price and deadlines stay in our hands.

CNC precision

Computer-controlled cutting and drilling means clean joints and doors that line up.

3D before you commit

Change the layout, colours and handles on screen — not after production has started.

Hardware that lasts

Soft-close hinges and runners, moisture-resistant boards and durable edge banding.

Two decades of practice

Kitchens, wardrobes and interiors delivered across North Macedonia since 2002.

CNC services

Bring us your drawing. We will cut it.

Beyond our own furniture we take on CNC work for carpenters, shopfitters and businesses — cutting, milling, drilling, engraving and 3D lettering in wood-based boards, acrylic and PVC.
